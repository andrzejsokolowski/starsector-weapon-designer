[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Any colony of yours whose Orbital Works holds a Pristine Nanoforge gets a new option: Weapon designer.
Buildings upgraded from Orbital Works count too, like AoTD's Orbital Skunkworks and Fleetwork Facilities, and so does AoTD's Tenebrian Nanoforge.
Design a weapon, save it, and build it through custom production like any other blueprint.
Needs LunaLib.
[/b][/center]
[center]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Designing[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]Size:[/b] small, medium or large.[/li]
[li][b]Mount:[/b] ballistic, energy or missile.[/li]
[li][b]Damage:[/b] kinetic, high explosive, energy or fragmentation.[/li]
[li][b]Projectile:[/b] ballistic shots, laser bolts, a beam, or missiles.[/li]
[li][b]Stats:[/b] damage, rate of fire, range, flux per damage, shot speed, spread, turn rate and EMP. Missiles get salvo size, reload, ammo, ammo regeneration, speed and guidance instead.[/li]
[li]Stats have no upper limit. Go as absurd as you like. Only spread stops, at perfect accuracy.[/li]
[li]They go down to the absolute minimums too: 1 damage, 10 range, 10 shot speed, 1 degree of turn rate, 360 degrees of spread.[/li]
[li]Fire rates beyond what the game fires by itself are made up with extra projectiles on every shot, and past a point with harder hitting ones, so even absurd rates keep the battle running.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Scripts[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]Arc discharge:[/b] hull hits throw lightning arcs at the target.[/li]
[li][b]Shield breach:[/b] shield hits throw arcs that pass through the shield.[/li]
[li][b]Detonation:[/b] every hit explodes and damages everything nearby except allies.[/li]
[li][b]Armor breaker:[/b] hull hits deal extra high explosive damage.[/li]
[li][b]Shield overload:[/b] shield hits push extra hard flux into the target.[/li]
[li][b]Leech:[/b] hull damage dealt repairs the firing ship.[/li]
[li][b]Engine jammer:[/b] hull hits slow the target for 3 seconds, up to 99% slower.[/li]
[li][b]Point defense:[/b] the AI fires the weapon at missiles and fighters first.[/li]
[li]Every script is bought in levels, most with no upper limit.[/li]
[li][b]Borrowed scripts:[/b] take the on-hit, on-fire, every-frame or beam effect of any loaded weapon, base game or modded, that fires the same way. A borrowed script that does not work on your design switches itself off in battle instead of crashing.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Design points and OP[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every stat step and script level costs design points. Each button shows exactly how many.[/li]
[li]10% of a design's design points becomes its OP cost: 100 design points make a 10 OP weapon.[/li]
[li]A design that copies a base game gun costs about what that gun costs.[/li]
[li]The game refuses to mount weapons of 1000 OP or more, so bigger designs cost 999.[/li]
[li]LunaLib settings: the design points to OP rate, the production price, an optional OP limit per weapon, and the price of borrowed scripts.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Building and editing[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]A saved design joins your custom production at its listed price.[/li]
[li]Pick its look from any loaded weapon of the same size and projectile, modded ones included. It takes that weapon's sounds too.[/li]
[li]Write your own description for the weapon's tooltip.[/li]
[li]Designs can be edited later. Every copy already built changes with it.[/li]
[li]Up to 10 designs per projectile type, 40 in all.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Saves[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Safe to add to a game in progress.[/li]
[li]Removing it from a campaign with designed weapons breaks that save, like any weapon mod.[/li]
[/list]
[/center]

[spoiler=Version log]
[b]0.4.0[/b]
[list][li]Borrowed tab: take scripts (on-hit, on-fire, every-frame and beam effects) from any loaded weapon of the same family, base game or modded. A borrowed script costs half its weapon's OP in design points (LunaLib setting) and switches itself off in battle if it does not work on the design.[/li]
[li]Stats go down to the absolute minimums: 1 damage, 10 range, 10 shot speed, 1 degree of turn rate, 360 degrees of spread, and longer reloads and higher flux. Going down is never blocked; design points can drop below 1.[/li]
[li]Fixed: designs worth 1000 OP or more were "not installable" in every slot, because the game refuses such weapons. They now cost 999 OP, and the designer says so.[/li][/list]
[b]0.3.2[/b]
[list][li]Fire rate has no top again. Above 30 shots a second, every real shot brings extra projectiles to make up the designed rate. Past 6 per shot (2 for missiles), the projectiles hit harder instead. Extra shots cost flux and ammo like real ones.[/li][/list]
[b]0.3.1[/b]
[list][li]Letters typed with AltGr (Polish keyboards) now work in names and descriptions. Letters the game's fonts lack become plain ones, and curly quotes and long dashes from pasted text become plain ones.[/li]
[li]Very long words in a description are split so the text wraps.[/li]
[li]Shots per second stops at 60 and seconds between missile salvos at 1/60: the game fires a weapon at most once per frame, so higher values were paid for and never fired.[/li]
[li]Engine jammer stops at level 28 (99% slower).[/li]
[li]Shield breach respects ship effects that resist shield piercing, like the base game's arcs.[/li]
[li]Very high script levels on fast guns no longer draw hundreds of arcs and blasts in one frame. Past 40 per frame the damage still lands, without the visuals.[/li]
[li]Detonation uses the base game's lighter explosion and flak sound.[/li]
[li]A copy costs at most 10,000,000 credits, so large production orders cannot overflow the total.[/li][/list]
[b]0.3.0[/b]
[list][li]Stats are bought with design points. 10% of them becomes the weapon's OP cost (LunaLib setting). Every step shows its exact cost, never 0.[/li]
[li]No more OP limit per size, and stats have no upper limit.[/li]
[li]Scripts: arc discharge, shield breach, detonation, armor breaker, shield overload, leech, engine jammer and point defense, bought in levels.[/li]
[li]Edit the weapon's description.[/li]
[li]LunaLib settings: design points to OP rate, production price, optional OP limit per weapon. LunaLib is now required.[/li]
[li]10 blank weapons per projectile type instead of 100, so the weapon list is no longer flooded.[/li][/list]
