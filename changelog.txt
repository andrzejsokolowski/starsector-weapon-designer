Version 0.4.1 (2026-11-10)
- New gold icon in mod managers (TriOS) and in LunaLib's settings.

Version 0.4.0 (2026-10-26)
- Borrowed tab: take scripts (on-hit, on-fire, every-frame and beam effects) from any loaded weapon of the same family, base game or modded. A borrowed script costs half its weapon's OP in design points (LunaLib setting) and switches itself off in battle if it does not work on the design.
- Stats go down to the absolute minimums: 1 damage, 10 range, 10 shot speed, 1 degree of turn rate, 360 degrees of spread, and longer reloads and higher flux. Going down is never blocked; design points can drop below 1.
- Fixed: designs worth 1000 OP or more were "not installable" in every slot, because the game refuses such weapons. They now cost 999 OP, and the designer says so.

Version 0.3.2 (2026-10-26)
- Fire rate has no top again. Above 30 shots a second, every real shot brings extra projectiles to make up the designed rate. Past 6 per shot (2 for missiles), the projectiles hit harder instead. Extra shots cost flux and ammo like real ones.

Version 0.3.1 (2026-10-26)
- Letters typed with AltGr (Polish keyboards) work in names and descriptions. Letters the game's fonts lack, curly quotes and long dashes become plain ones.
- Very long words in a description are split so the text wraps.
- Shots per second stops at 60 and seconds between missile salvos at 1/60: the game fires a weapon at most once per frame.
- Engine jammer stops at level 28 (99% slower).
- Shield breach respects ship effects that resist shield piercing, like the base game's arcs.
- Very high script levels on fast guns no longer draw hundreds of arcs and blasts in one frame. Past 40 per frame the damage still lands, without the visuals.
- Detonation uses the base game's lighter explosion and flak sound.
- A copy costs at most 10,000,000 credits, so large production orders cannot overflow the total.

Version 0.3.0 (2026-10-25)
- Stats are bought with design points. 10% of them becomes the weapon's OP cost (LunaLib setting). Every step shows its exact cost.
- No more OP limit per size, and stats have no upper limit.
- Scripts: arc discharge, shield breach, detonation, armor breaker, shield overload, leech, engine jammer and point defense, bought in levels.
- Edit the weapon's description.
- LunaLib settings: design points to OP rate, production price, optional OP limit per weapon. LunaLib is now required.
- 10 blank weapons per projectile type instead of 100.

Version 0.2.1 (2026-10-25)
- The Tenebrian Nanoforge from AoTD - Vaults of Knowledge unlocks the designer too.

Version 0.2.0 (2026-10-25)
- Buildings upgraded from Orbital Works, like AoTD's Orbital Skunkworks and Fleetwork Facilities, open the designer.
- Pick a design's look from any loaded weapon of the same size and projectile.
- Fixed a crash when typing a design name.
